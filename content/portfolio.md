Here are some of the websites I've built or worked on. My work has included coding, design, copy editing, and search engine optimization (SEO). Each entry below links to the live site.

## Nonprofits and Community Groups

### Newburgh Creates

<a class='underline' href='https://newburghcreates.com'>newburghcreates.com</a>

A nonprofit in Newburgh, NY that runs hands-on STEM and art programs for kids. I rebuilt the site with Hugo, a static
site generator (it had been written by hand in HTML), and I handle its ongoing development and maintenance along with the SEO strategy. I also contribute to the copywriting and the blog. The design is by Jason Otero.

### Newburgh Creates Community

<a class='underline' href='https://www.newburghcreatescommunity.com'>newburghcreatescommunity.com</a>

A companion site for the families and teams in Newburgh Creates programs, with projects, team resources, and a blog. I
designed and built it with Hugo, and I added a content management system (CMS) that runs in the browser, so posts can be written and edited without
touching any code.

### STEM Venturing Crew 111

<a class='underline' href='https://stemventuringcrew111.org'>stemventuringcrew111.org</a>

The site for a Venturing crew in Newburgh, NY that's focused on science, technology, engineering, and math. I designed
and built it with Hugo. The logo was designed by the whole crew.

### The Newburgh Project

<a class='underline' href='https://thenewburghproject.org'>thenewburghproject.org</a>

A project to revitalize Newburgh through architecture, art, technology, gaming, and history. Jason Otero designed the
site, and I built his design with Hugo.
